03 / BACKGROUND

Designed for the real world.

I'm an electronics systems engineer with professional experience at Texas Instruments, one of the world's leading semiconductor companies. My focus is design, development, debugging, and validation of electronic systems for industrial, energy and automotive applications.

My work spans both PCB design and physical lab execution: schematic capture, circuit analysis, precision SMD assembly and rework, firmware troubleshooting, oscilloscope measurements and hardware bring-up.

I value engineering solutions that are clear, testable, reliable and well documented.

Independent personal portfolio. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Texas Instruments.
